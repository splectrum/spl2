# PROJECT PLAN - Glossary Term Requirements

**Project Code:** 06-glossary-term-requirements
**Project Type:** Exploration Project

---

## Products

### Product 1: Requirements/Self-eval Template
Initial format for glossary term requirements and self-evaluation criteria. Living artifact that evolves through Products 2-4.

### Product 2: DSL Glossary Terms
Requirements for all DSL glossary entries (~17 terms). Technical, precise definitions. Tests and refines template.

### Product 3: Stepping Stones Terms
Requirements for all Stepping Stones entries (~30+ terms including new ones). Conceptual, navigational patterns. Surfaces template challenges.

### Product 4: Spots Glossary Terms
Requirements for all Spots glossary entries (~5 terms). Organizational, activity-based. Validates template.

### Product 5: Spots Structure (Twin Pair)
**Implementation:** Spots requirements document, housekeeping concept, backlog restructuring (move to backlog/ spot)
**Pattern template:** Spots/housekeeping pattern for future spot creation

---

## Sequence

1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5

Template first, then glossaries in order of difficulty (easier → harder → validation), spots structure last.

---

## Dependencies

- Template (Product 1) informs all glossary work
- Products 2-4 refine template understanding
- Product 5 applies patterns learned from Products 1-4

---

## Notes

- **Collaboration mode:** COLLABORATIVE throughout (Exploration Project)
- **Template evolution:** Product 1 establishes initial format, evolves through Products 2-4, remains living artifact beyond project
- **Asymmetric twins:** Three glossary products share one template product
- **Addon scope:** Product 5 delivers spots requirements, housekeeping concept, backlog restructure

---

## Success Criteria

Per Exploration Project requirements:
1. Architecture validated - requirements format proven for conceptual patterns
2. Patterns established - template captures what works for term requirements
3. Evidence gathered - all existing glossary terms have requirements
4. Foundation laid - future terms can use established patterns
5. Lessons captured - learning synthesized for methodology evolution
