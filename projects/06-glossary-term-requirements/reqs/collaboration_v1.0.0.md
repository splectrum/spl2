# collaboration

## Spec

Joint activity that is interactive, a partnership with mutual respect aiming to deepen mutual understanding and focused on triggering increased insight.

Each party has different but also overlapping roles in the partnership.

Mandatory for exploration projects and partnership-critical moments.

Scope: Global.

Purpose: Achieve what neither party could alone through combined strengths and perspectives.

## Self-eval

- [ ] Interactive joint activity
- [ ] Mutual respect maintained
- [ ] Deepens mutual understanding
- [ ] Triggers increased insight
- [ ] Roles clear (different but overlapping)

## Comments

Core to HAICC methodology. Collaboration vs execution: know when to work together vs independently.
