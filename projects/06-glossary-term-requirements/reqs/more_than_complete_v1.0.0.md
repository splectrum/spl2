# more than complete

## Spec

For situations where complete exists somewhere but is difficult to figure out. Be overgenerous in approach to capture completeness within.

When you know the complete answer is there but hard to extract precisely, cast a wide net.

Scope: Global.

Purpose: Ensure completeness by overshooting rather than risking gaps.

## Self-eval

- [ ] Complete is known to exist
- [ ] Complete is hard to extract precisely
- [ ] Overgenerous approach taken
- [ ] Completeness captured within

## Comments

Contrast with:
- **minimal and complete** - complete isn't fully known
- **sufficient and complete** - capture essence with redundancy for preservation
- **more than complete** - complete exists, hard to extract, overshoot to capture
