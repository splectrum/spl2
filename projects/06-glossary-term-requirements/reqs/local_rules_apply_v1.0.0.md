# local rules apply

## Spec

Artifacts are self-sufficient in their requirements. No external supervisor, director, or orchestrator at a distance.

Each artifact:
- References its own requirements version
- Has its own self-eval criteria
- Can be assessed independently
- No retroactive burden when requirements evolve

The importance of self-eval: without it, you need external oversight. With it, the artifact is autonomous.

Scope: Global.

Purpose: Enable distributed, autonomous quality assessment. No central authority needed.

## Self-eval

- [ ] Artifact references specific requirements version
- [ ] Self-eval criteria are complete (no external input needed)
- [ ] Can be assessed independently
- [ ] No forced upgrades when requirements change
