## Key Observations

### Autonomy Trajectory

We're moving toward autonomy-by-default. The reqs need maturing first - can't be forced, must emerge through use. This project validated the pattern: start collaborative, establish understanding, then autonomy follows naturally.

### The "So Alike But So Different" Dynamic

The Product 3 calibration moment captured this well:
- **User intent:** Let's discuss approach, then execute
- **AI interpretation:** Approach similar to Product 2, batch execute

Not frustration - surprise at the interpretation gap. Same words, different mental models. This is the core challenge and opportunity of human-AI collaboration.

### Learning Through Doing

Adjustments happen organically, from experience on the ground. Pre-negotiating everything would be friction without benefit. Better to:
- Try
- Catch misalignment quickly
- Adjust
- Continue

"Be happy to fail at times and learn from it, it's worth it and it can be fun too" - this is the right stance for partnership evolution.

## Project Assessment

### What We Built

67+ term requirements across three glossaries:
- DSL: 24 terms (code-facing vocabulary)
- Stepping Stones: 35+ terms (methodology concepts)
- Spots: 8 terms (repository structure)

Unified structure: Term | Description | Req
Base concepts: api_node, activity, spot
Extends pattern: term names (not versions) for decoupled evolution

### Meta-Pattern Value

This wasn't just documentation - it's infrastructure. Every future glossary term has a template. Every concept can be pinned to requirements. Quality assessment becomes possible.

### Deferred Wisely

Product 5 (Spots Structure Twin Pair) deferred to combine with tooling. Backlog restructuring, housekeeping concept - all deferred. These are related; better as coherent future work than scattered incomplete scope.
