# Risks - Project 06: Glossary Term Requirements

Risk tracking throughout project lifecycle.

---

### R01: Template Instability
**Identified:** 2025-11-18
**Probability:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium

**Description:** Template evolves significantly through glossary work, causing rework on earlier terms.

**Mitigation:** Accept as natural pattern. Earlier terms use earlier template version (local rules apply). Can update if value justifies effort.

---

### R02: Scope Creep from Meta-Nature
**Identified:** 2025-11-18
**Probability:** High | **Impact:** Medium

**Description:** Defining requirements for requirements invites philosophical depth that expands scope.

**Mitigation:** Stay practical. Requirements must be testable/validatable. If we can't verify it, it doesn't belong.

---

### R03: Large Volume Creates Fatigue
**Identified:** 2025-11-18
**Probability:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium

**Description:** ~50+ terms is substantial. Quality may degrade through fatigue or rushing to complete.

**Mitigation:** Work in batches. Take breaks between glossaries. Quality over speed. Collaboration mode maintains accountability.

---

### R04: Conceptual Terms Resist Requirements
**Identified:** 2025-11-18
**Probability:** Medium | **Impact:** High

**Description:** Some stepping stones may be too abstract for meaningful requirements/self-eval.

**Mitigation:** This is the exploration - discover what works. Document what doesn't work as equally valuable learning.

---

### R05: Backlog Restructuring Breaks References
**Identified:** 2025-11-18
**Probability:** Low | **Impact:** Medium

**Description:** Moving backlog/ spot may break existing references throughout repository.

**Mitigation:** Systematic search and update. Test all references after move.

---
