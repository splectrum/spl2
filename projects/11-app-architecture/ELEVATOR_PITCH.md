## Angles to Organise

### AI Partnership

- AI as **implementation actor**, not just assistant
- Two modes: collaborative (human + AI together) and autonomous (AI works independently)
- Human sets direction, AI implements
- Tooling emphasis - practical creation, not theoretical
- AI as primary user of the platform (DSL designed for AI consumption)
- Reduce friction between intention and implementation

### AI Autonomy Vision

- **Human + AI together at the top** - collaborative creation, equal partners
- **AI autonomy below** - manages and evolves lower levels
- More than one AI role: creative partner AND autonomous implementer
- At creative level: AI is as important as human - **1 + 1 = 11**
- Autonomous level: **constrained creativity** - initiative within boundaries
- Lower levels become AI territory to manage AND evolve
- Trust AI to improve, refactor, optimise within its domain
- As layers mature, human+AI focus moves higher, more becomes autonomous

### The Stack Vision

```
AI Layer         → Partnership, creation, autonomy
Conventional     → "Stuff we know" patterns and solutions
P2P Layer        → Pear/Bare foundation, decentralised
```

- Bridge AI capabilities to P2P infrastructure
- Conventional solutions in between (familiar, accessible)
- Not reinventing application patterns - bringing them to P2P

### Platform Characteristics

- Pattern-driven, not orchestration
- Data patterns as keys that unlock doors
- Local rules apply in layered contexts
- Flow: exploration → patterns emerge → APIs crystallise → DSL connects
- Mycelium web: links through entrypoints, self-contained contexts
- Free scripting and formal implementation in same environment

### Mycelium Web / Repo Vision

- Repository structure designed for **AI and P2P first**
- Feels natural to AI - pattern-based navigation, self-describing structures
- Feels natural to P2P - distributed, no central authority, local rules
- Less natural to humans at first sight - but humans adapt
- Entrypoint linking, not direct internal access
- Self-contained contexts with clear boundaries
- Information flows like nutrients through mycelium - finds its way

### DSL Engine - Vertical and Horizontal

- **Vertical**: Layering of DSL - from wrappers and base APIs upward
  - Each layer builds on the one below
  - Abstraction ladder human+AI climb together
- **Horizontal**: Common vocabulary at conceptual/creative level
  - Shared language across domains
  - Same terms mean same things
  - Enables AI and human to think together
- DSL as the connective tissue - both directions

### Stepping Stones - Equally Connective

- DSL connects the technical layers
- Stepping stones connect the conceptual layer - how you **talk about** domains
- Navigational concepts that appear at different decision points
- Same stones, different contexts - choose which to step on
- Shared mental models, not just shared terms
- Two vocabularies working together: DSL (doing) + Stepping stones (understanding)

### Howto + Mycelium = Our Orchestration

- No central orchestrator - but things still coordinate
- **Howto glossary**: entry points to procedures (how to do X)
- **Mycelium web**: structure to navigate through
- Together: guided behavior without central control
- Pattern-driven coordination, not command-driven
- The "orchestration" emerges from structure + vocabulary
- **Domino effect**: triggers cascade across layers
- No "commands from above" - consequences flow from patterns
- Each layer responds to what arrives at its entrypoint
- Coordination through resonance, not hierarchy
