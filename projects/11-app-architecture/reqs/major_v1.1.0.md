## Spec

1st part of version; for disruptive upgrades.

Breaking changes that require attention from consumers.

### Character

- First position: X.y.z
- Disruptive: may break existing usage
- Significant: major evolution

## Self-eval

- [ ] Indicates breaking/disruptive change

## Comments

Part of semver pattern: major.minor.patch
