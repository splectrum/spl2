## Spec

Meandering is productive exploration through discussion. What appears as tangent or inefficiency is actually valuable discovery - letting conversation explore surfaces better designs that efficient direct paths would miss.

The pattern: start with one question, let discussion flow naturally, arrive at insights that neither party anticipated. The journey itself creates value.

Key characteristics:
- Exploration over efficiency
- Tangents welcomed, not suppressed
- Each step builds on previous insights
- Destination often different (better) than starting assumption
- The friction is productive friction

Opposite of: direct path execution, "getting to the point", efficiency-first thinking.

Related to: collaboration, twin pair methodology, haicc.

## Self-eval
