## Spec

3rd part of version; for minor fixes.

Bug fixes and small corrections that don't add features.

### Character

- Third position: x.y.Z
- Fix: corrects existing behavior
- Minor: small change

## Self-eval

- [ ] Indicates bug fix or minor correction

## Comments

Part of semver pattern: major.minor.patch
