# Chat Capture Requirements

**Purpose:** Preserve insights from free-flowing collaborative discussions that would otherwise be lost.

**Pattern:** Sufficient and complete

- Capture essence of conversation with overlap/redundancy
- Prefer capturing too much over missing key insights
- Contrast with "minimal and complete" (which accepts gaps)

**What to capture:**
- Collaborative working-through-ideas discussions
- Quick clarifications that inform decisions
- Terminology discussions and reasoning
- Insights that emerge from informal conversation
- Decisions made outside formal project artifacts

**Who:** AI summarizes (before memory is lost)

**When:** During or at end of chat, while context fresh

**Format:** Natural language narrative

- Readable, conversational
- Not rigid structure
- Tell the story of the discussion

**Naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD_topic-description.md`

**Location:** `/chats/`

---

**Note:** This is embryonic. Will evolve through use. First chat captured: terminology discussion (2025-11-13).
