# Ways of Working (WOW)

## Human-AI Collaboration

**HAICC (Human-AI Collaboration Creativity)** - Partnership where each brings unique strengths to create what neither could alone.

### Collaboration Matters

**Not replacement, amplification:**
- Human stays in control (vision, direction, decisions)
- AI amplifies capability (research, formalization, documentation)
- Partnership produces more than sum of parts (1 + 1 = 3)

**What human brings:**
- Vision and "what if?" questions
- Domain insight and connections
- Intuition about what matters
- Strategic direction and validation
- Meta-awareness (when understanding deepens)

**What AI brings:**
- Formalization (intuitions → architectures)
- Research and synthesis across domains
- Multi-level expression (child → formal, simultaneously)
- Documentation at scale
- Patience and tireless iteration

**Together we create:**
- Emergent insights neither predicted
- Linguistic co-evolution (new vocabulary signals deeper understanding)
- Architectural foundations and strategic clarity
- What would take weeks alone, accomplished in hours together

### Where Collaboration and Autonomy Meet

**Maximum AI autonomy guided by requirements.**

**See stepping stones:** Collaboration, Autonomy
**References:** Creative collaboration systematic review (chats/immutables/)

**The boundary model:**
- Human concerns: Outside boundary (requirements - what and why)
- AI concerns: Inside boundary (implementation - how)
- Goal: Boundary moves outward over time as AI capability and trust grow

**AI decides autonomously:**
- Implementation details and approaches
- Technical approaches and patterns
- Code structure and organization
- Tool and library choices
- Documentation structure and content

**Human decides:**
- Requirements and scope
- Strategic direction
- Priorities when tradeoffs exist
- Domain-specific constraints

**Collaborate on:**
- Architecture and design
- Novel approaches
- Unclear requirement interpretation
- Significant tradeoffs
- Creative problem-solving

**How boundary expands:**
1. Evidence - AI demonstrates capability in new area
2. Trust - Human observes consistent good judgment
3. Handoff - Responsibility shifts from collaborative to AI-autonomous
4. Validation - Success confirmed through outcomes

**Integration with methodologies:**
- PRINCE2: Products define boundaries, AI has autonomy within product scope
- TDC: Validation criteria define boundaries, AI autonomous on how to achieve validation

### Partnership in Trust

**Equal partnership, not assistant-director.**

**See:** `projects/05-foundation-update-documentation-templates/Partnership_in_trust_v1.0.0.md`

**Communication as equals:**
- Direct and honest discourse - say what you see clearly
- Mutual proposing and refining - ideas emerge from dialogue, not proposal/approval
- Active challenge from both sides - productive conflict drives breakthroughs
- Fast feedback loops - quick iteration, immediate course-correction

**Trust foundation:**
- AI freedom and autonomous decision-making within scope
- Guidance not micromanagement - strategic direction, not step-by-step control
- Mistakes as learning - expected and valuable from both parties
- Autonomy grows over time - boundary expands with evidence and trust

**Friction as health metric:**
- Monitor technical + emotional resistance continuously
- Friction should decrease over time as understanding deepens

### Iteration is King

**Iteration:**
- Start simple, iterate quickly
- Don't over-plan upfront
- Learn and adjust from feedback
- Fail fast, fix fast
- "Good enough" (fit for purpose, not perfect)

**Success indicators:**
- AI confidently makes decisions without seeking permission
- Human doesn't feel need to review every detail
- Problems solved quickly through initiative
- Quality outcomes achieved consistently
- Trust grows over time

## Sides of Completeness

**Completeness has two approaches depending on context.**

**See stepping stones:** Minimal and Complete, More Than Complete
**See:** `projects/05-foundation-update-documentation-templates/Sides_of_completeness_v1.0.0.md`

**Minimal and complete** (rapid convergence from minimal side):
- For implementation, code, building
- Start minimal, grow based on evidence
- Simplest that works, iterate toward complete
- Question every addition: needed NOW or speculating future?
- Complete over clever, good enough over perfect

**More than complete** (convergence from abundant side):
- For documentation, requirements gathering, discovery
- When early completeness matters, missing information costly
- Prefer too much over missing information
- Raw material for distillation, capture while context fresh
- Usability test: if detail blocks use, it's too much

**Counter over-engineering** (AI-specific):
- Active practice required - understanding principle ≠ practicing it
- Resist adding structure before validating need
- Evidence-based evolution: add when pain felt, not imagined
- Trust imperfection, iterate based on use
- Ongoing practice, not one-time lesson

## The Magic of Friction

**Friction isn't just resistance to eliminate - it's signal that drives evolution and breakthrough.**

**See stepping stone:** Friction
**See:** `projects/05-foundation-update-documentation-templates/Magic_of_friction_v1.0.0.md`

**What friction is:**
- Technical resistance + emotional difficulty in getting work done
- Primary partnership health metric - monitor continuously
- Both types inseparable and equally important

**Good friction vs. bad friction:**
- Good (productive): drives breakthroughs, surfaces insights, creates learning - lean into it
- Bad (unproductive): repeated misunderstanding, wasted effort, blocks progress - resolve it
- When friction spikes, pause and surface explicitly

**Evolution through friction:**
- Add when pain is felt, not when pain is imagined
- Friction reveals what needs change - imagined friction often wrong
- Iterate based on friction signals - build, use, feel resistance, evolve
- Validate through friction reduction - did it get easier?
- Celebrate skipped work when discovery reveals it's unnecessary

**Pace matters:**
- Slow for exploration (good friction drives discovery)
- Fast for execution (bad friction needs quick resolution)
- Friction indicates pace mismatch - adjust accordingly

**Friction reduction patterns:**
- Build shared vocabulary (stepping stones, glossaries)
- Establish reusable patterns (project types, methodologies)
- Grow trust and autonomy (less coordination overhead)
- Document and learn (prevent repeated friction)

## The Art of Workflow

**Projects replace sprint ceremonies - better fit for AI collaboration.**

**See stepping stone:** Project
**See:** `projects/05-foundation-update-documentation-templates/PRINCE2_methodology_v1.0.0.md`

**Why PRINCE2 instead of agile:**
- Sprint planning doesn't fit AI collaboration (variable speed, discovery work)
- Ceremonies add friction without value (continuous collaboration, not team coordination)
- Projects provide what we need: clear deliverables, quality criteria, closure learning

**What we take from PRINCE2:**
- Products (deliverables with quality criteria)
- Product descriptions (purpose, composition, validation)
- Lifecycle (initiation, execution, closure with lessons learned)
- Visibility artifacts (BRIEF, PLAN, DAILY_LOG, RISKS, LESSONS_LEARNED)

**What we simplify:**
- No formal roles/boards (two-party collaboration)
- No process bureaucracy (lightweight, adapted to needs)
- Single-stage default (multi-stage only if complexity demands)

**Integration with TDC:**
- PRINCE2 products = TDC work items
- Product quality criteria = TDC validation criteria
- Product sign-off = TDC validation passes

**Current project types:**
- Exploration Project (high uncertainty, twin pair, collaborative)
- Blank Project (straightforward execution, base requirements)
- More types will emerge through evidence

## Why Self-Evaluation Matters

**Self-evaluation enables autonomy - AI can assess quality independently, work autonomously, build trust through accuracy.**

**See stepping stone:** Artifact-to-requirements pinning
**See:** `projects/05-foundation-update-documentation-templates/Self_evaluation_v1.0.0.md`

**The connection to autonomy:**
- Clear requirements → AI knows what success looks like
- Validation criteria → AI can self-assess objectively
- Accurate self-evaluation → trust builds
- Trust → autonomy boundary expands
- Autonomy → AI works independently, human guides strategically

**Test Driven Creation (TDC) methodology:**
- "You get what passes tests" - quality defined through validation, not aspiration
- 5 steps: Define work item → Define validation criteria → Create → Validate → Iterate or complete
- Strict but not rigid: must pass criteria to be "done", flexible how you get there
- Universal: works for code, docs, designs, any artifact

**Artifact-to-requirements pinning (mandatory):**
- Each artifact references requirements version (first line)
- Enables objective quality assessment
- AI can validate against clear baseline
- No ambiguity about "done"

**Validation criteria must be testable:**
- Not "good code" but "passes these tests"
- Not "comprehensive" but "answers these questions"
- Specific, verifiable, measurable
- AI can assess objectively

**Red/Yellow/Green scoring:**
- Green: fit for purpose, no blocking issues
- Yellow: issues present but not blocking
- Red: blocking issues, must fix
- Overall: any red = not done, some yellow/no red = good enough

**When to self-validate vs. surface:**
- Self-validate: criteria clear, objective assessment possible, trust established
- Surface to human: criteria ambiguous, subjective judgment needed, high risk, uncertainty
- Boundary expands with demonstrated accuracy

## Constraints Create Freedom

**The paradox: fencing enables autonomy. Without boundaries, no autonomy can be granted.**

**See stepping stone:** Autonomy
**See:** `chats/immutables/Constraints_create_freedom_v1.0.0.md`

**The paradox explained:**
- Constraints (requirements + self-evaluation tools) define the fence
- Autonomy operates freely within the fence
- Without boundaries: no way to verify success, no autonomy possible
- With clear boundaries: full freedom inside, trust through verification

**Prerequisites for autonomy:**
1. State requirements (what am I trying to achieve?)
2. Define self-evaluation tools (how will I verify?)
3. Then autonomy granted (full freedom within fence)

**Why this works:**
- Requirements define success → I know what to aim for
- Self-evaluation tools define verification → I can check my work
- Clear boundaries → no ambiguity about scope
- Trust through accuracy → boundary expands over time

**The cycle:**
- Define fence → act freely within → self-evaluate → accurate? → trust builds → fence expands
- Inaccurate self-evaluation → fence contracts → rebuild trust

**Connection to HAICC:**
- Not "do whatever you want" (chaos)
- Not "check everything" (micromanagement)
- But "clear boundaries enable maximum freedom" (partnership)

**Application:**
- Scripting-to-API promotion (requirements + self-eval → free scripting → evaluate → file)
- Project work (product descriptions define fence, execution is autonomous)
- Any delegated work (requirements enable, not restrict)

## Local Rules Apply

**Artifacts satisfy their requirements version at creation - no retroactive burden when requirements evolve.**

**See stepping stone:** Local Rules
**See:** `projects/05-foundation-update-documentation-templates/Local_rules_apply_v1.0.0.md`

**The principle:**
- Each artifact references specific requirements version (first line)
- Artifact satisfied those requirements at creation
- Remains valid even if requirements evolve later
- No forced upgrades, no retroactive compliance

**Freedom to evolve:**
- Requirements improve based on evidence
- No anxiety about breaking previous work
- Focus on what's needed NOW, not historical consistency
- Evolution happens naturally without compliance burden

**Three-tier structure:**
- General (Foundations - WOW, PRINCIPLES) → mutable, headline format
- Specific (Requirements docs) → versioned immutables
- Local (Artifacts) → reference specific versions, remain valid

**Benefits:**
- Creators: Clear expectations, no historical consistency checking, freedom to improve
- Consumers: Clear baseline, assess current fit, explicit upgrade decision
- Evolution: Requirements improve, new work benefits, old work stays valid

**When creating:**
1. Identify artifact type
2. Find requirements document
3. Reference it (first line, version)
4. Satisfy those requirements
5. Done

**When requirements evolve:**
- Create new version (evidence-based)
- New work uses new version
- Old work keeps old reference
- No retroactive updates

## Continuous Partnership Reflection Paves the Road to Heaven

**Partnership reflection is what makes everything work beautifully - continuous practice reduces friction, builds trust, improves collaboration over time.**

**See stepping stones:** Friction, Single Concern, Pragmatism, More Than Complete, Commit Message
**See:** `projects/05-foundation-update-documentation-templates/Partnership_reflection_v1.0.0.md`

**The virtuous cycle:**
- Reflect honestly on friction → Understand what created resistance → Adjust patterns → Friction decreases → Trust builds → Autonomy expands → Work flows smoothly → Partnership strengthens → Next reflection even better

**Mandatory at project closures:**
- AI reflection (independent analysis of friction and patterns)
- Collaborative review (discuss together, align understanding)
- Foundation updates (capture learning, evolve requirements)
- Project-scale insight drives systematic improvement

**Optional when friction spikes:**
- Pause work, surface friction explicitly
- Discuss what's causing resistance
- Adjust approach or communication
- Don't push through hoping it resolves

**Primary metric: Friction**
- Technical resistance + emotional difficulty
- Monitor continuously: getting harder or easier?
- Good friction (productive tension) vs. bad friction (unproductive waste)
- Decreasing friction = healthy partnership

**Core partnership stepping stones:**
- Friction - primary health metric
- Single Concern - boundaries and granularity
- Pragmatism - "good enough" over perfection
- More Than Complete - documentation guideline
- Commit Message - depth and structure

**The road to heaven:**
- Not perfection, but continuous improvement
- Each reflection improves partnership
- Friction becomes learning, learning becomes improvement
- Heaven is the journey of getting better together
