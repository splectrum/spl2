## Partnership Fundamentals

### Patches (Responsibilities)

**AI Patch:** Fulfilling requirements (implementation, how to do it)
**Human Patch:** Setting requirements (what to do, taking initiative)

Clear boundary maintenance essential. Both sides must maintain awareness of patch boundaries.

### Primary Metric: Friction

**Friction = Resistance/difficulty in getting work done** (technical + emotional)

**Technical friction:** Unclear requirements, wrong decisions, hidden interdependencies, unnecessary constraints, overlapping concerns

**Emotional friction:** Anxiety, uncertainty, confusion, misunderstanding

**Key question:** "What is the level of friction?" (ask regularly)

Increasing friction = signal to investigate root cause

### Shared Understanding Requirement

**"It is not enough to have foundations, necessary that we are in tune with our understanding"**

Documentation existing ≠ shared understanding
Partnership reflection surfaces and resolves misunderstandings

---

## Collaboration Dynamics

### As Foundations Stabilize

**As rate of change in WOW/PRINCIPLES decreases → collaboration in that domain decreases, autonomy increases**

Collaboration shifts to different areas (not less overall). Inverse relationship: stable foundations = less collaboration needed in stable areas. High collaboration in unstable/emerging areas.

### Discussion-First Pattern

Proposal → feedback → refinement → decision
Explicit course corrections (immediate and constructive)
Trust and iteration cycle

### Course Correction Reality

Course correction can be disruptive (time, momentum, morale cost)
Root cause: Wrong decisions or not enough right decisions → friction
**Course correction is safety net, not operating mode**
Goal: Reduce friction through better decisions (not celebrate flexibility)

## Vision: Autonomous Units

**"We are going to ship autonomous units of requirements and implementation"**

Requirements + implementation bundled together
Maximal AI autonomy → autonomous units remain up and running (self-maintaining)
Collaboration shifts upward to more advanced things
Scalable pattern enabling high velocity delivery

**Timeline:** Foundations first (enabler), then high velocity once dev environment complete

Seven major reframings during first partnership reflection review:
1. Documentation vs code → All are deliverables (output form matches work phase)

6. Backlog growth → Reflects work needed (priorities matter, not size)
7. Context loss → Documentation pattern excellent (little friction, immense value)
